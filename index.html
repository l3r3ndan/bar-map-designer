How it works

From sketch to skirmish

Making a BAR map used to mean a chain of specialist tools. Here it is one window.

Shape the battlefield

Drag on landforms, draw ridges with passes and rivers with fords, add ramps up to base plateaus. Symmetry mirrors the other team's half for you, live.

Make it look real

Pick a biome, paint ground textures, beaches and liquid, scatter BAR's own trees and rocks, set the sky, weather and time of day, and fly around it in 3D.

Build & play

One click compiles the map, installs it into BAR and boots it in the game's engine to check it loads and every metal spot is detected. Then start a skirmish.

Features

Everything a map needs

Drag-and-drop terrain

Mountains, plateaus with crisp rims, terraced hills, tiered land, gorges with land bridges, cliff lines, volcanoes, meteor craters, lakes, rivers and ramps that show their slope.

Paint anything

Draw rivers and mountain ranges freehand, paint plateaus, basins, lakes and islands in any shape, fill whole areas with one click, plus beaches, liquids, grass and forests.

Fair random maps

Pick biome, layout, ground shape and set piece, or Surprise me. Clear routes between bases, every area reachable, and every generated feature stays editable.

Edit in 3D

Every tool works directly on the 3D terrain: place shapes, paint and sculpt while the land updates around you, with BAR's real models and lighting.

Real textures

A built-in library of 75 photo-scanned surfaces (CC0), or import your own images and normal maps.

Sky & weather

Rain, snow, ash, dust and spores that can come and go, clouds, fog, mist over acid lakes and time of day.

Gameplay controls

Metal spots and geothermal vents, wind and tidal energy, liquid damage, gravity, terrain hardness.

Getting started

  1. Install Beyond All Reason and start it once so it downloads the game.
  2. Run the BAR Map Designer installer.
  3. Open it and press New: blank canvas, gentle terrain or a random map.
  4. Shape it, then press Build & install.
  5. In BAR, start a Skirmish and pick your map.
FAQ

Questions

Is this made by the Beyond All Reason team?

No. BAR Map Designer is an independent, unofficial tool made by a BAR player. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the Beyond All Reason project.

Can I play my maps online?

You can play them in Skirmish straight away. BAR's online rooms only offer maps from BAR's official map list, so for online play submit your map for review in the BAR Discord's #mapping channel. The designer prepares a review pack for that.

Do I need World Machine, GIMP or PyMapConv?

No. The designer writes BAR's map formats itself, including terrain, textures, metal, features, sky and the map's settings file. You can still import a heightmap from World Machine or Gaea if you like.

Can I share the maps I make?

Yes. The bundled textures are public domain (CC0), and maps use BAR's own trees, rocks and effects, so nothing in them needs extra permission.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your PC. The editor runs on your own computer, never online, and your maps are plain files in your Documents folder.

Mac or Linux?

Windows first. A Linux version is planned; macOS isn't, since Beyond All Reason itself doesn't support it.
